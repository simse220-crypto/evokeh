*Evokeh** is a display typeface rooted in the word *evoke*.
Its name gathers the initials of Essence, Value, Origin, Knowledge, Emotion, and Harmony — or Human.

Beginning with hand-drawn sketches, Evokeh sets out to bring back the human senses and emotions behind every letter, even in an age of fast-changing technology.

The family consists of five styles: Solid, Contour, Outline, Hairline, and Construction. Built on a single skeleton, each style unfolds the letter from mass to line and from line to structure, revealing the many layers within a single form.
